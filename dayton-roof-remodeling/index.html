About us

The oldest roofing company in the Miami Valley

Dayton Roof & Remodeling started in 1947 repairing and installing roofs. Over the years our services have grown to cover the full exterior of your home or building: roofing, siding, gutters, windows and doors.

Owner Chuck Davis and our installers are certified by CertainTeed. We have held an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau, accredited since 1955, and we source materials locally whenever possible.
