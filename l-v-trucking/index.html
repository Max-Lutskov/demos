About us

Who we are

L.V. Trucking Inc. started as a local cartage company in the early 1970's and has grown into an intra-state and regional carrier based in Columbus, Ohio. Our growth comes in part from loyal customers we still serve since the start of the business.

We believe in being loyal to our customers. Once we make a commitment to you, we will honor that commitment. Our drivers return home daily, giving us low turnover and dependable on-time performance.
