About Gem City Door

Gem City Door is a family-owned and operated garage door company based in the Dayton area. We handle residential and commercial work, from repairs and spring conversions to new doors, openers and full design services.

We offer start-to-finish solutions for every garage door project and stand behind high-quality service and customer satisfaction on each one.
