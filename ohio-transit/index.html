Why shippers choose us

Why ship with OTC

Zero-tolerance safety

Our first priority is that every driver and owner-operator goes home safe, backed by a rigorous zero-tolerance safety protocol.

Thorough fleet inspections

Every September we inspect every unit down to every tire, on top of yearly, quarterly and daily electronic pre- and post-trip checks.

Driver coaching and tracking

An in-house coaching program uses real dash cam examples to teach defensive driving, and we monitor the fleet with real-time GPS tracking.

Rest and compliance

A recognition program rewards drivers who consistently take proper rest and breaks.

About us

Built in Ohio on old-school values

Founded in 2013 by Mohamed Ali, Ohio Transit Company is built on acting with integrity and delivering strong service to clients and brokers alike.

We began with over-the-road produce transport. Growing steadily, we now run about 200 highway units, including trucks and trailers, while keeping a personal approach. We also support our community through outreach and charitable work.
