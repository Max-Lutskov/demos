FAQ

Questions customers ask

How do I choose the right type of fence or gate for my property?

It depends on privacy, security, looks, maintenance and budget. Wood or vinyl suit residential privacy; chain link, aluminum or ornamental steel suit commercial security. We will recommend a solution.

How long does fence or gate installation usually take?

A standard residential fence often takes a few days or less. Larger commercial or custom jobs may take a week or more. We provide a realistic timeline with your quote.

Can you handle large commercial projects?

Yes. With decades of experience and a professional crew, we handle projects of all sizes, from small home installs to large commercial security fences.

Do you offer gate automation and repair, or just installation?

We offer full-service gate automation, installation and repair, including sliding, swing and driveway gates and maintenance of existing gate openers.

What maintenance do fences and gates need?

Wood needs occasional sealing or staining. Vinyl, aluminum and ornamental steel need cleaning and occasional inspection. Automated gates should have mechanical parts checked periodically.

Do I need a permit to install a fence or gate?

It depends on location, use, height and type. We review local codes and zoning before each project and can help you obtain any required permits.
