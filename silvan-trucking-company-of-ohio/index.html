About us

Who we are

S & T Truck and Auto Service, Inc. dba Silvan Trucking Co. of Ohio is a Columbus-based carrier providing intrastate and interstate contract carriage within a 600 mile radius of Columbus. Founded in 1986, we specialize in dedicated services to key accounts.

We operate 34 power units with 93 dry van and tanker trailers. Our work is guided by integrity, honesty, character and loyalty.
